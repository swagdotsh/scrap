Scrap 1.5

Now Playing
- See exactly how artist and album names will be scrobbled when the first-artist or album-suffix cleanup options change them.
- Listening counts, artist biographies, top songs, and top albums now follow the first-artist setting and refresh when it changes.

Settings
- Added examples for the first-artist and album-suffix cleanup options.
- Added an editable “Keep these artist names intact” list, with Earth, Wind & Fire included by default, to protect band names containing commas or ampersands.

Menu bar
- “Love track” now uses a native checkmark to show whether a track is loved. Select it to toggle love status.
- “Share track” now opens the native macOS sharing picker with the track’s Last.fm link.
