## Reports
- Added a **Reports** page with your weekly report (Saturday to Friday): scrobbles compared with the previous week, your top artist, album and track, and the new artists, albums and tracks you discovered.

## Notifications
- Get notified every 5,000 scrobbles, when you discover a new artist, and when your weekly report is ready every Friday at 16:00 Central European Time. Clicking a notification opens the matching page.
- Turn each one on or off in **Preferences → General → Notifications**.

## Profile
- Choose the period for your top artists, albums and tracks: last 7 days, 30 days, 365 days or all time.
- **See more** opens a window with your top 50 for that chart and period.

## Now Playing
- Added animated album covers, with an option to prioritize fetching them.

## Scrobbles
- Shows what you're playing on another device, according to last.fm, and refreshes every 30 seconds while open.

## Friends
- Fixed friends always showing no recent listening. Each friend now shows their latest scrobble and whether they're listening right now.

## Settings
- Added **Remove “(feat.)” and “(with)” from song titles**.
- Added **Include (Apple Music Edition)** to the album suffix cleanup.

## Other
- The session key is no longer sent in request URLs.
- scrap remembers the page you were on.
